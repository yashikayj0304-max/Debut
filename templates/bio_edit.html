🔗 Link in bio

What is this?

Instagram lets you put only one link in your profile. This gives you that one link, and it opens a small page of all your links in one place: your shop, WhatsApp, latest offer, a contact form.

  1. Fill in the boxes below and Save.
  2. Copy your link (or print the QR code).
  3. Paste it in your Instagram bio, under Edit profile → Links.

Example: a bakery adds “Order on WhatsApp”, “Today's menu” and “Find us on Maps”. We count the taps on each so you see what people want.
