Quick answers
Where is everything?
🏠 Home (top left) goes to your dashboard. Side-panel headings open and close like dropdowns. Click your name (top right) for Profile, Send feedback and Log out; your role (Owner, Admin, Manager, Lead…) shows under it. ❓ opens these guides and 🔔 your notifications.
How do I invite my team?
Team & roles → type or paste as many emails as you like (commas, spaces or new lines) and pick one role for all of them. For different roles per person, download the CSV template, fill it in and upload it. Pending invites can be revoked — it shows in the Activity log.
How do I send a mood board to someone?
Open the board → 🔗 Share. Copy the team link (sign-in needed) or create a public view-only link anyone can open. You can make a new link or turn it off any time.
I already have a free account — what happens when my company adds me?
Your own free workspace stays exactly as it is. Accepting the company's invite adds you to the company workspace, where you get that workspace's plan and the role they gave you. Switch between them from the workspace name at the top left.
How does search work?
Type in the search box (top bar) and press Enter to search everything. The ✕ in the box or Esc clears it, and “✕ Close search” on the results page takes you back. Ctrl+K (⌘+K on Mac) jumps to the box from anywhere.
What do I fill in for Experiments?
Change one thing, measure one number. Hypothesis: “If we [change], then [result], because [reason]”. Channel, Success metric (the one number you watch), Variant A (what you do now), Variant B (the one change), Status, and after the test the Result / decision.
How do I make a tracking link?
Campaigns > UTM Builder > ＋ New UTM link. Fill the form, watch the finished link build, press Copy.
What do the roles mean?
Owner and Admin run everything; Lead and Manager work day to day; Creators see only their own work (no prices); Viewers read only; Clients only see their portal. The full table is in the user manual.
How do I start?
Follow the “Get started” checklist on your Home screen. Each step opens the right page.
What should I actually do each day?
Click Home (top left) — the "Today" list at the top auto-builds itself from what's overdue plus what's due today, so you don't have to check every module by hand. For the fuller picture (brand consistency, posting consistency, campaign spend, leads) with a full checklist of gaps, see Marketing Health.
Where did the "More tools" popup go?
It's gone for good — every module now shows directly in the sidebar. Pop-ups are kept only for things that genuinely depend on another panel, like "Need an idea?" inside a new Content Item.
Does {{ C.APP_NAME }} post to LinkedIn or Instagram for me?
No. You plan and draft here, post on the platform yourself, then mark the item Published.
Why is a menu item locked (🔒)?
It's part of a higher plan. You can see everything you have; nothing is deleted if you stay on Free.
AI says I've used my credits.
Credits reset each month and depend on your plan. Verify your email to use AI.
How do I add a teammate?
Team & roles > Invite someone (Team and Agency plans have more seats). Use the lowest role that works.
How do I get my data out?
“Export data” in the menu downloads everything as spreadsheets. You can also delete your workspace under Account.
I lost my phone (two-factor).
Use one of your recovery codes on the code screen, or contact us to reset it.
Still stuck?

Use Send feedback (it includes the page you were on) or see our Contact page.
