1. Sign up (about 2 minutes)

  1. Open Create account. Enter your name, work email, a workspace / company name (up to 80 characters; your team sees it at the top of the app) and your industry.
  2. Leave Load my industry starter kit ticked to get personas, a launch checklist, key dates and news feeds for your industry. You can add or delete all of it later.
  3. Choose a password. It needs at least 8 characters, one UPPERCASE letter and one special character (for example ! @ # $ %). A strength bar and checklist below the box turn green as you meet each rule, and the 👁 icon shows or hides what you typed. Easy-to-guess passwords (your email name, “password”, “123456”, long repeats) are refused.
  4. Tick the Terms / Privacy box and press Create account. You land on your Home screen with a {{ C.TRIAL_DAYS }}-day trial of every feature. After that you keep the Free forever plan; nothing is deleted. No card needed.
  5. Verify your email from the link we send (the yellow banner has a Resend button). AI features and some settings need a verified email.

Were you invited? Use the link in your invitation email. The page shows the company and your role, your email is already filled in and locked, and you only set a name and password. Already have a Debut account? Log in with the same email and accept the invite: the company workspace is added next to your own free one, which stays exactly as it is. Switch between them from the workspace name at the top left.

3. Your first week

  1. Day 1: follow the “Get started” checklist on Home: write a GTM plan, add content to the calendar, create a mood board, add an influencer, capture a lead, invite a teammate, verify your email. The bar at the top of Home fills as you go.
  2. Day 2: open Strategy > Brand Kit and add your colours, fonts and voice. In Account you can scan an existing brand deck to pull them in.
  3. Day 3: fill the Calendar for the next 2 weeks (use the Idea Bank when stuck).
  4. Day 4: set a target in KPIs and log the first numbers.
  5. Day 5: invite your team and set the right roles.
  6. Every day: open Home and work through the “Today” list. Once a week check Growth > Marketing Health for gaps.

4. Adding and editing things

  • Every “New” and “Edit” opens a pop-up form over the page, so you never lose your place. Save returns you to the same page.
  • Each form starts with what it is for. Fields marked * are required; a field’s hint is shown under it. Some fields allow several choices (for example an influencer’s platforms are tick-boxes).
  • Lists show a Budget used bar for campaigns and promotions; it turns red when spend passes the budget.
  • Lists have Export to Excel and a blank Excel template, so you can fill in bulk data and import it. Drag the ⠿ handle to reorder cards on boards.
  • Deleting asks you to confirm. Everything you do is recorded in the Activity log (owners/admins can export it as CSV).

Tracking links (UTM Builder) and Experiments

  • UTM Builder (Campaigns): press ＋ New UTM link. A pop-up asks for a label, the page you are linking to, where the link will be posted (source, for example instagram), the channel type (medium, for example social) and the campaign. The finished link builds itself as you type; press Copy and paste it into your post, ad or email. Saved links are listed with a Copy button.
  • Experiments (Campaigns) is for A/B tests: change one thing, measure one number. Fill in: Hypothesis (“If we [change], then [result], because [reason]”), Channel, Success metric (the one number you will watch, for example saves or click-through rate), Variant A (what you do today), Variant B (the one thing you change), Status (Idea, Running, Concluded) and, once finished, Result / decision (what happened and whether you keep, drop or retest it).

5. Calendar

  • Shows content by publish date and your key dates (festivals, sales, events) in orange. Use ◀ ▶ for months and the channel filter at the top.
  • Press ＋ in any day to add content for that date. On a computer, hover a day to see shortcuts to the Idea Bank and Mood Boards.
  • Drag a chip to another day to reschedule it.
  • Use the private link under the title to see the calendar in Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar. Keep it secret; press Reset link if it leaks.

6. Mood boards

  • Start from a template (product launch, festive, social campaign and more) or a blank board. Drop images, add colour swatches, notes and links, and drag everything into place.
  • Buttons on a board: Share, Rename, Duplicate and Save colours (adds the board’s colours to your Brand Kit).
  • Share gives two kinds of link: a team link (people must sign in) and a public view-only link that anyone can open without an account. You can make a fresh link or turn the public link off at any time. Public pages are hidden from search engines.

Inviting people

  1. Open Team & roles > Invite someone.
  2. Several people, one role: paste as many emails as you like (separated by commas, spaces or new lines), choose the role and press invite. Each gets their own link.
  3. Different roles: download the CSV template, fill in email and role (and client name for clients), upload it. Rows with problems are listed with the reason; good rows still go through.
  4. Pending invites show on the page. Revoke cancels one; it is written to the Activity log. Invites stop at your plan’s seat limit.

8. Clients and the portal

Invite someone with the Client role and type their client name exactly as you use it in the “Client” field of your content. They get a clean portal with their own content to approve, request changes on or comment on, plus their campaigns. They cannot see other clients, prices, your team or any other page. Use Preview next to a client on the Team page to see what they see. You can also send a read-only Client report link.

10. Make it yours: colours

Go to Account. Pick a primary colour (your brand colour) and a secondary colour. The secondary colour is yours to choose: it colours buttons, the active menu item, your avatar, the Create button, the dashboard banner and highlights, blended with your primary. Press Use auto to let Debut choose a matching lighter shade. Upload your logo in the same place. The preview strip shows the combination before you press Apply.

11. Account and security

  • Password: 8+ characters, one capital, one special character; change it under Account. Resetting uses an emailed link.
  • Two-factor login: turn it on in Account with an authenticator app. Save your recovery codes; one of them lets you in if you lose your phone.
  • Verify email: unlocks AI features and sensitive settings.
  • Your data: “Export data” downloads everything as spreadsheets; owners can delete the workspace under Account.

12. News, Market Pulse and AI

  • Latest Updates shows live news from your feeds. If your server cannot reach the internet it shows a saved snapshot dated 1 Nov 2026 instead, and you can search it.
  • Market Pulse follows trends, videos and competitors for your industry (competitor watch-list size depends on your plan).
  • AI Studio drafts calendars, plans, blogs and reports. Each use costs credits from your monthly allowance. Debut does not post to social platforms for you: you post, then mark the item Published.
