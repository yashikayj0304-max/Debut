❓ How mood boards work
  • Add anything — use the buttons above the canvas to drop in an image (upload or paste a URL), a sticky note, a colour swatch, or a link.
  • Move it — press and drag any card anywhere on the canvas. It saves instantly, no save button needed.
  • Resize it — drag the little triangle in a card's bottom-right corner.
  • Bring to front — just click or drag a card; it automatically layers above the others.
  • Edit it — double-click a card to change its text, colour or link in place. Double-click an image card to swap the picture itself.
  • Zoom & pan — use the − / + buttons, type a percentage in the Zoom box and press Enter (or Ctrl/Cmd + scroll) to zoom the canvas, and scroll or drag the background to pan around a big board.
  • Tidy up — the "🧹 Tidy up" button re-stacks every card into a neat grid if things get messy.
  • Not sure where to start? — go back to All boards and click "✨ Load a sample board" to see a worked example you can edit or delete.
