🖼️ Mood Boards

Collect images, colours, notes and links into visual boards for each campaign or launch.

🧭 How it works

A mood board is a free-form, drag-anywhere canvas — like a Miro board, scoped to your workspace. Every card saves itself the moment you move, resize or edit it; there's nothing to "save" separately.

  1. Create a board below, then add images, notes, colour swatches and links from the toolbar on it.
  2. Drag, resize, double-click to edit — every card is fully editable in place, including swapping a picture with a new one.
  3. Zoom in/out on a board to work on a big, busy canvas, just like Miro.

New to mood boards? Click "✨ Load a sample board" above for a ready-made example — four card types already placed, so you can see the format before building your own. Every board also has its own "❓ How to use" button with the full walkthrough.
