What we collect

Account data (name, email, password hash), workspace content you enter (plans, calendars, contacts of creators/influencers/leads, uploaded images), billing status (payments are handled by Razorpay; we do not store card details), and technical logs (IP address, browser, actions in an audit log).

Why we use it

To provide and secure the service, bill you, send transactional emails (verification, password reset, invites), prevent abuse, and improve the product.

Your customers' and contacts' data

You are the controller of personal data about people you store in your workspace (for example creators, influencers, leads). You must have a lawful basis to store and contact them. We process it only on your instructions to run the service.

Connected social accounts

If you connect Instagram, Facebook or LinkedIn, we store an access token so we can publish the posts you schedule, plus the account name and id so you know which account you are posting to. For posts we published we also store their reach, likes, comments, saves and shares, so your Numbers page can show how they did. We never read your direct messages, your followers' details, or anything about the people who interact with your posts. Disconnecting an account in Publishing deletes the token immediately.
