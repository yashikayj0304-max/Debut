Connect an Instagram account

Instagram only lets apps post to Business or Creator accounts that are linked to a Facebook Page, and it only accepts JPEG pictures (Debut converts its own designs for you). In the Instagram app: Settings → Account type and tools → Switch to professional account, then link your Page.

Open Meta's Graph API Explorer, pick your app, tick instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish and pages_show_list, generate the token and paste it here. Debut swaps it for a long-lived one, so you only do this once.

Connect LinkedIn

LinkedIn is separate from Meta and needs its own free app at linkedin.com/developers, with the w_member_social permission. Posting to a company page also needs the Community Management API, which LinkedIn reviews.
