FAQ

What happens when the trial ends, or I cancel?
You drop to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted and you can always export everything. Paid-only features lock, and if you are over a Free limit you can still view, edit and delete, just not add more.

What are AI credits?
Each AI generation uses 1-3 credits (larger tasks cost more). Credits reset monthly.

What is group mode?
On Team and Agency plans you can appoint a Team Lead who sees and edits everything, uses the Team hub (workload, approvals) and manages the team, without access to billing or workspace deletion. Creators can send work for review but not approve it.

Is there an API?
Yes, on Team and Agency: API keys with read or read-and-write access to every module (push KPIs from analytics, create leads and content). The lead-capture form and webhook are on every plan.

Is it tailored to my industry?
Yes. Choose personal brand, D2C, FMCG, B2B, SaaS, startup, hospitality, retail, real estate, education, healthcare, fashion, fintech or agency and your workspace gets a matching starter kit, AI tasks and the right modules.

Can freelancers and clients get access?
Yes. Invite people as Manager, Creator (freelancers: limited access, no prices or costs) or Viewer (read-only, internal stakeholders) or Client (portal only). Clients on Team and Agency get a private client portal (their own work only: approve or request changes, and comment), and you can also send a read-only report link.
