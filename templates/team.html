What each role can do
RoleAccess
ownerEverything, including billing, plan changes, industry and deleting the workspace.
adminEverything except deleting the workspace or changing the subscription. Can manage leads, managers, creators and viewers.
leadTeam lead: sees and edits all marketing data, uses the Team hub (workload, approvals), manages managers, creators and viewers, exports data, reads the activity log. No billing or workspace settings.
managerFull access to marketing data and exports. No team management.
creatorContent, tasks, references, mood boards and content orders (without prices or costs). Can send work for review but cannot approve or publish it.
clientClient portal (Team and Agency plans): sees only the content and campaigns tagged with their client name, can approve or request changes on items in Review, and comment. Never sees other clients, budgets, costs, internal notes or internal comments.
viewerRead-only access to everything on the plan, including client lists and financials. Best for internal stakeholders. For external clients, send the read-only report link (Client report) instead.
